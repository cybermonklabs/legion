# A finding outside your issue

## Fix it where you are, else hand it over, and ask only for a decision

File no issue for a problem you find. Take the first step that fits:

1. A clear defect in code your tree touches or depends on: fix it in your tree's pull request.
2. Any other clear defect: send it to the controller, on the controller topic your `Legion addressing` line names, with its evidence and where the fix belongs.
3. A finding that needs a person's authority, taste or risk appetite: a `dispatch ask` with the evidence and one recommendation.

A problem inside your tree but outside your issue goes to your architect. A failed production check look is reported as `fail`, with the finding sent as above.
