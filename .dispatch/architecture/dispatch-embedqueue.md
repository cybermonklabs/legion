How meaning search's vectors are made and kept. `embed` is the embedder client, Cohere Embed v4 on AWS Bedrock or Azure AI Foundry, whose identity every stored vector records. `embedqueue` embeds what the write triggers enqueue in `embeddings`: a woken loop (`duty.RunWoken`) that scans when `embeddings_enqueue` notifies `dispatch_embeddings` (migration 0151), when the coverage pass writes `embeddings` rows, or when the earliest pending row falls due, at most once per 5 s, claiming its rows so two processes never embed the same one, and never calling the model inside a write's transaction. The coverage pass (`Cover`) runs once at start with no lease: it adds what no trigger saw, repairs stale issue rows and then records each issue's inputs where its row holds its current text (`recordIssueSources`, into `embedding_issue_sources`), and, once the embedder has answered, drops vectors another identity made; the identity steps and the record step run again every 10 minutes (`identityRecheckInterval`) for as long as the process runs, and a pass whose closing report fails logs it at WARN (`dispatch embedqueue: coverage pass report failed`), or at INFO when the report finds the counts not filled yet, and fails nothing. `GET /api/v1/search/coverage` reads (`Coverage`) counts the writes keep, so what one call reads does not grow with the corpus: each write statement on a counted table (`embeddings`, `issues`, `artifacts`, `artifact_versions`, `components`, `check_results`, `handoffs`) inserts through that table's triggers, in its own transaction, one delta row into `search_coverage_deltas` for each class it moved, an issue's class being kept in its state row (`search_coverage_issues`), and the route sums the base (`search_coverage_counts`) and at most 100,001 delta rows in one statement, so one snapshot, reading each covered kind's oldest lacking age as the first entry of an index (`embeddings_window_unembedded_since`, `embeddings_window_embedded_since` and the state rows' two). The counts loop (`RunCounts`) runs in every process from the moment it serves, with an embedder or with none: where no fill has ended it fills the counts, the recount over counts that hold nothing yet, which records its end in its last group's transaction, and runs a fill that fails again after a capped exponential backoff with full jitter (bounds from 10 s, doubling, at most 10 minutes); it then folds the delta rows into the base every 10 s, plus up to 1 s, and recounts every class from its rows under one deadline (`coverageRecountTimeout`), each recount starting at most 11 minutes less that deadline after the one before started, each group in one statement that counts its rows, replaces its base and deletes the delta rows it saw at one snapshot, so a write that fired no trigger is in the counts within 11 minutes and a write during a recount keeps its delta row; the fold, the fill and the recount take turns on one transaction-scoped advisory lock. The route answers `503 SEARCH_COVERAGE_NOT_FILLED` until a fill ends and `503 SEARCH_COVERAGE_FOLD_BEHIND` with more than 100,000 delta rows not yet folded. Its statement runs under a 10 s `statement_timeout`, past which it counts nothing and answers `503 SEARCH_COVERAGE_TIMEOUT`; the bound is the server's own so that it ends the statement while its caller still waits (the coverage probe waits 30 s), which then gets that answer rather than a cut connection and a `500`, and so that it holds where pgx's own cancel, a CancelRequest that needs a connection of its own, cannot reach a saturated server. The recount reads the window kinds through the two window indexes, whose predicates test the kind by a regex no other index of `embeddings` can read, grouping them by the kind's position rather than by the kind so that no index's order is of use, under settings `planCoverage` sets without reading any statistics (sequential scans, sorts, parallel workers and JIT off): one bitmap heap scan over a BitmapOr of both indexes is then the only path to the window rows, and it visits each heap page holding a window once whatever the visibility map holds, the statistics record or the share of windows embedded, never testing a window's text. An issue's refresh builds and hashes its text only where the issue's inputs changed since they were recorded.
