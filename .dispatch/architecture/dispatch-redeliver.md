The sweep that asks GitHub to redeliver the App webhook's failed deliveries, which GitHub never redelivers itself. At start and then about every two minutes, each wait the interval less up to a tenth of it drawn afresh every time (`duty.JitteredWithin`), so no wait is longer than two minutes, under the `webhook-redelivery` lease (`duty.Exclusive`, once per period of 0.9 of the interval across the processes, `duty.LeasePeriod`), it lists the webhook's failed attempts as the App (`dispatch-auth`), judges each delivery by its GUID, and requests a redelivery of each the listener answered 5xx or GitHub could not complete, on a doubling backoff (2, 4, 8, 16, 32, then 60 minutes), each next request due nine tenths of its backoff after the sweep that made the one before so the sweep that many intervals on makes it, until GitHub records an `OK` attempt for it or 72 hours have passed since the first failed attempt the sweep saw. Its cursor, one record per delivery acted on, one index entry per open delivery — keyed `pending.<YYYYMMDD>.<HH>.<MM>.<GUID>` by the minute that delivery next needs a sweep — and the marker that says every open record has an entry live in the JetStream KV bucket `envoy_webhook_redelivery`, reached through the listener's bus client (`envoy-listener`); its rate-limit backoff lives in Postgres (`github_rate_limits`, scope `redelivery`). The running sweep reads from that index the deliveries due in its window, rather than every record the bucket holds, and lists the last hour and six minutes (`maxBackoff` plus three intervals), long enough that a delivery coming due still sees GitHub's answer to its last request in the listing. A delivery's GUID, event and action are GitHub's text, so every line and error of the sweep shows them, the bucket keys that hold a GUID, and the bucket's errors, which quote those keys, as `githubapp.ShownBody` shows GitHub's text: whole up to 512 runes, and past that the first 512 and the size. A delivery whose GUID the bucket cannot key (outside nats' key alphabet, holding a space or an empty token, or so long that its index entry's key passes a write's bound, which `bus.KeyValue.RefusesWrite` answers) is left out of every sweep that lists it, each time with one WARN line, `webhook redelivery left out a delivery whose GUID the bucket cannot key`, and the sweep goes on and stays complete. `envoy-dispatch redeliver-webhooks` runs the same sweep over a chosen window, reading every record in the bucket rather than the index.
