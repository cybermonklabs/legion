**How it is measured.** `delivery.ListUnownedP0` lists the project's issues that have priority 0, a status other than done, no claim and no route. It reads them through the partial index `issues_open_p0`, which holds the open P0 issues alone, with the claim and the route a filter over what it reads, so the count reads no issue that is not an open P0. Each refresh writes the count as the row of that UTC day, with the time of the refresh as its last value's time where the count moved (`WriteUnownedP0Day`); a refresh that finds the count unchanged leaves the row, and the time it holds, as they were. A window's value is the count of its newest day (the `last` fold). The Delivery page and this eval count by the same function.

**What it does not count.** Issues that are done, issues of a lower priority, and issues that hold a claim or a route.
