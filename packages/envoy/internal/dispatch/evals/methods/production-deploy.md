**How it is measured.** For each UTC day, `measures.DeployJobsDay` reads the runs of the deploy workflow on main that a push started on that day. This eval follows the jobs of the production rule of the delivery settings, which lists a renamed job's old name and its new name, so the history and the new runs both count. Each run adds at most one result, from its production job: the earliest listed job to complete with success as a production deploy, or else the first listed job. Where the rule names an apply step, a job that succeeded while that step did not succeed applied nothing, so it reads as skipped and is not counted. The result is the job's outcome, and its minutes from its start to its completion when it ended in success or failure. A timed-out job counts as a failure. A cancelled job is counted beside the times and is not timed. `measures.FoldTiming` merges the minutes of a window's days. It answers their count, their median and their 90th percentile to one decimal, the count of each outcome, and the success share: the successes over the successes and the failures. The Pipeline section and this eval call the same fold.

A run counts for the project of its repository.

**What it does not count.** Jobs that applied nothing, runs in which the job did not run or is still running, runs on other branches, and runs of other workflows.
