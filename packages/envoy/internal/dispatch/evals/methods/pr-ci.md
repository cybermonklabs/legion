**How it is measured.** For each UTC day, `measures.PRChecksDay` reads the runs of the PR-checks workflow that a pull request started on that day. For each run that ended in success or failure, it takes the wall time from the run's start to its completion. A timed-out run counts as a failure. A cancelled run is counted beside the times and is not timed. A run with any other conclusion, or one that is still running, adds nothing until it concludes. `measures.FoldTiming` merges the minutes of a window's days. It answers their count, their median and their 90th percentile to one decimal, the count of each outcome, and the success share: the successes over the successes and the failures. The Pipeline section and this eval call the same fold.

Each job of a run that ended in success or failure has a series of its own, `job:<name>`, when the job itself ended in success or failure. Its minutes are the job's duration, rounded to whole seconds. The job series are unlisted: the list of evals does not fold them, and the items route answers them. One series row, `job:*`, holds the unit, the fold and the minimum count of every job series. `measures.FoldSlowestJobs` ranks the five slowest jobs by their median over the days shown. A job needs 50 timed results in those days to be ranked (`measures.SlowestMinResults`, the `min_n` of `job:*`).

A run counts for the project of its repository, and so does each of its jobs.

**What it does not count.** Runs of other workflows, runs that something other than a pull request started, cancelled runs in the times, and the jobs of a run that did not end in success or failure.
