**How it is measured.** The eval counts the project's issues that finished in the window. An issue finished when it closed with Legion phase work: an event for its own key by a session titled `Legion <role> · <KEY>`, or a handoff. Its work time is the hours from its first worker event to its close, less the hours it sat parked in `backlog` or `icebox`, less the hours it sat back in the queue after its work started, and less the hours it was out of the workflow before its close. The first worker event is the first event of such a session, or the first handoff of an issue whose Legion work is handoffs alone.

An hour out of the workflow before the close is one in which the daemon observed the issue in `done` while Dispatch had not closed it: a merge before Dispatch's status write, a reopened issue's `done` stretch, or a child that left the workflow, such as one moved to `triage`. Before the daemon observed the issue, it is the stretch from an `issue.closed` that a reopen follows, or from a `triage` status write, to the next status write. Such an hour is outside the work time, as a parked hour is, and it is no parked hour: the parked hours are the hours in `backlog` or `icebox` alone. When the daemon stops reporting an issue, Dispatch's whole read of the daemon's state writes `null` for every field of the issue at one moment, and that is a drop, not a release. The phase and the slot that the issue held run on through a drop until the next observation that names a phase, and from that moment the issue holds a slot only if a slot observation at the same moment names one; where no such observation comes, they run to the issue's end.

`legionfacts` computes the work time of each issue by the rules of `legion/metrics`, and stores it in `issue_facts`. For each UTC day, `metrics.WorkTimeDay` takes the work time of each issue that closed that day. `metrics.FoldWorkTime` merges the hours of a window's days. It answers their count, median and 90th percentile (`measures.Quantile`), rounded to four decimals, and never the mean of the daily medians.

The eval has three series, cut by the stored root flag: `hours` holds every issue, `hours:roots` the issues that are the root of their tree, and `hours:children` every other issue.

**What it does not count.** Open issues, issues that closed with no Legion phase work, hours in `backlog` or `icebox`, hours back in the queue after the work started, hours out of the workflow before the close, and hours before the first worker event. No hour of slot wait is in it.
