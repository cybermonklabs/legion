Before you change a symbol, or brief a worker to, ask the `codegraph` tool what the change reaches and which tests cover it, and grep only for what it does not answer: `write` to `xd://codegraph` the content `{"action":"impact","symbol":"<name>"}`, the same with `"action":"callers"`, and `{"action":"affected","files":["<path>"],"depth":1}` for the test files the paths you change reach.
