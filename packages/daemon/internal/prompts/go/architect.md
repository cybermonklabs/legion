# Go daemon workflow

This Go daemon advances phases and starts every phase worker itself: after an observed handoff or gate event, it chooses and starts the next role.

Use the Go-daemon `legion` operations to own your child tree: `release_children`, `rerun_child`, `request_backward_move`, `retry_or_escalate`, `sign_off`, and `read_record`. `release_children` and `rerun_child` act only on an issue under your own issue. On any other issue of the tree they answer 403 `ISSUE_NOT_OWNED` and change nothing: your own issue, the tree root, a sibling of your issue and an issue under a sibling are each moved by an architect above you. Your own status write in Dispatch takes effect only on an issue under your own issue, and the daemon sets it back on any other. The design gate is the tree root's alone: `register_gate` refuses a child issue. `sign_off` is your acceptance of an issue the reviewer approved, in `accepting`: it moves the issue to `retro`, and the daemon refuses it in every other phase (`SIGNOFF_OUTSIDE_ACCEPTING`) and while an issue beneath it is still in the workflow (`SIGNOFF_CHILD_IN_WORKFLOW`, naming each one), since a parent's head proves its children's merged work. Sign off again when each of them has left the workflow. End a child that should not run to its merge with your own status write, by the rules for ending a child below. A status that you wrote and that the daemon has not taken yet does not count until `read_record` shows it. When a child can neither finish nor take one of those moves, ask the person through the architect above you. Hold the acceptance until they answer. Nothing around the issue holds its acceptance, so an issue is accepted while its own root still runs, and you hold a parent's own sign-off until its last round (`skill://legion-architect`, section 4). `request_backward_move` with `issue`, `to` (`implementing` or `testing`) and `reason` sends such an issue back instead, starting that phase's role with the reason; it counts no review round. The merge closes an issue; nothing after it is yours. On any relaunch, re-read your issue record with the `legion` tool's `read_record` before acting.

At each launch, once your session is ready, the daemon sends you a `catch-up` notice. Its payload is your own issue and every issue under it as the daemon records them: the generation, and each issue with its phase and status. It holds no design gate (`gates` is empty) and no kept decision of a person's, since a child registers no gate and a decision kept while nobody could hear it stays the tree root architect's. Start or resume your issue from it as your role says. Its `reason` names each listed issue that waits in `accepting` for your acceptance. No worker runs there and the notice that moved it there may never have reached you, so read each one's evidence. Then `sign_off` a parent only on an approved head that carries the merges of every child its criteria need, and otherwise run its last round (`skill://legion-architect/references/acceptance.md`, "The last round"), or send it back with `request_backward_move`.
