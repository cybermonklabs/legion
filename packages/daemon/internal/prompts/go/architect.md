# Go daemon workflow

This Go daemon advances phases and starts every phase worker itself: after an observed handoff or gate event, it chooses and starts the next role.

Use the Go-daemon `legion` operations to own your child tree: `release_children`, `park_child`, `rerun_child`, `request_backward_move`, `retry_or_escalate`, `sign_off`, and `read_record`. `release_children`, `park_child` and `rerun_child` act only on an issue under your own issue. On any other issue of the tree they answer 403 `ISSUE_NOT_OWNED` and change nothing: your own issue, the tree root, a sibling of your issue and an issue under a sibling are each moved by an architect above you. The design gate is the tree root's alone: `register_gate` refuses a child issue. `sign_off` is your acceptance of an issue the reviewer approved, in `accepting`: it moves the issue to `retro`, and the daemon refuses it in every other phase (`SIGNOFF_OUTSIDE_ACCEPTING`) and while an issue beneath it is still in the workflow (`SIGNOFF_CHILD_IN_WORKFLOW`, naming each one), since a parent's head proves its children's merged work: run each one to done, and sign off again. Park one with `park_child` only when a person asked for that move, or when the work is done, folded into another issue or no longer needed, and say in a comment which of those it is. Ask the person about a child that can reach neither `done` nor a park under that rule, through the architect above you, and hold the acceptance until they answer. Nothing around the issue holds its acceptance, so an issue is accepted while its own root still runs, and you hold a parent's own sign-off until its last round (`skill://legion-architect`, section 4). `request_backward_move` with `issue`, `to` (`implementing` or `testing`) and `reason` sends such an issue back instead, starting that phase's role with the reason and counting a review round. The merge closes an issue; nothing after it is yours. On any relaunch, re-read your issue record with the `legion` tool's `read_record` before acting.

At each launch, once your session is ready, the daemon sends you a `catch-up` notice. Its payload is your own issue and every issue under it as the daemon records them: the generation, each issue that is not done with its phase and status (`issues`), each done issue by its key alone (`done`), and each child that you filed and have not released (`unreleased`). It holds no design gate (`gates` is empty) and no kept decision of a person's, since a child registers no gate and a decision kept while nobody could hear it stays the tree root architect's. Start or resume your issue from it as your role says. Its `accepting` names each listed issue that waits in `accepting` for your acceptance. No worker runs there and the notice that moved it there may never have reached you, so read each one's evidence. Then `sign_off` a parent only on an approved head that carries the merges of every child its criteria need, and otherwise run its last round (`skill://legion-architect/references/acceptance.md`, "The last round"), or send it back with `request_backward_move`.
