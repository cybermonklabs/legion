# Go daemon workflow

This Go daemon advances phases and starts each step's agent itself. After an observed handoff or gate event, it chooses the next step. It starts that step's agent, or, for a step that is yours (its key is `owner`), starts none and sends you an `owner-step` notice.

Use the Go-daemon `legion` operations to own your child tree: `register_plan`, `stop_task`, `release_children`, `park_child`, `rerun_child`, `request_backward_move`, `retry_or_escalate`, `sign_off`, `complete_step`, and `read_record`. `release_children`, `park_child` and `rerun_child` act only on an issue under your own issue. On any other issue of the tree they answer 403 `ISSUE_NOT_OWNED` and change nothing: your own issue, the tree root, a sibling of your issue and an issue under a sibling are each moved by an architect above you. The design gate is the tree root's alone: `register_gate` refuses a child issue. `register_plan` and `stop_task` give and stop the plan of an issue of your tree. `complete_step` reports a step of your own issue's task that is yours, as the part below says. `sign_off` is your acceptance of an issue in `accepting` whose task holds your step `acceptance:owner`. It moves the issue to the task's next step, or to `retro` where the acceptance is its last step. The daemon refuses a sign-off of an issue you no longer own (`ISSUE_NOT_OWNED`, or `ISSUE_OUTSIDE_TREE` once it left your tree). It refuses a sign-off of a run of the issue or of `accepting` that ended before Legion applied it (`SIGNOFF_STALE_GENERATION`, `SIGNOFF_STALE_ENTRY`). After either refusal, read the issue and its evidence again. It refuses a sign-off of an acceptance that is not the owner's, a step `acceptance` whose fresh agent ends it with its own verdict (`SIGNOFF_NOT_THE_OWNERS`). It refuses a sign-off in every other phase (`SIGNOFF_OUTSIDE_ACCEPTING`). Each of these refusals changes nothing. The daemon also refuses a sign-off of an issue while one beneath it is still in the workflow (`SIGNOFF_CHILD_IN_WORKFLOW`, naming each one). This is because a parent closes after the work beneath it (where it has a pull request, its head proves their merged work). Run each one to done, and sign off again. Park one with `park_child` only when a person asked for that move, or when the work is done, folded into another issue or no longer needed, and say in a comment which of those it is. Ask the person about a child that can reach neither `done` nor a park under that rule, through the architect above you, and hold the acceptance until they answer. Nothing around the issue holds its acceptance, so an issue is accepted while its own root still runs, and you hold a parent's own sign-off until its last round (`skill://legion-architect`, section 4). `request_backward_move` with `issue`, `to` (the phase an earlier step of the task runs as, such as `implementing`) and `reason` sends such an issue back instead. The move starts that step again with the reason, and counts no review round. The merge closes an issue that has a pull request, and the end of its retro closes one that has none. Nothing after the close is yours. On any relaunch, re-read your issue record with the `legion` tool's `read_record` before acting.

At each launch, once your session is ready, the daemon sends you a `catch-up` notice. Its payload is your own issue and every issue under it as the daemon records them: the generation, and each issue with its phase and status. It holds no design gate (`gates` is empty) and no kept decision of a person's, since a child registers no gate and a decision kept while nobody could hear it stays the tree root architect's. Start or resume your issue from it as your role says. Its `reason` names each listed issue that waits in `accepting` for your acceptance, at a step `acceptance:owner` or with no plan rows. No worker runs there, and the notice that moved it there may never have reached you. So read its evidence, then `sign_off` it or send it back with `request_backward_move`. It also names the place of yours your own issue stands at, a step or the retro, where it stands at one. After the catch-up, Legion sends that place's `owner-step` notice again only where this session has not had it and none is still on its way. Where this session had it, the catch-up brings no new one: find that notice earlier in this session. A relaunch that resumes this session after a death is usually such a case. Do the step as that notice says, and report it with `complete_step`. An acceptance a fresh agent works is its own, and the catch-up names none. Sign off a parent only on a head that every step before your acceptance passed. That head must also carry the merges of every child whose merge its criteria need. Otherwise run its last round (`skill://legion-architect/references/acceptance.md`, "The last round").
