# Go daemon workflow

This Go daemon advances phases and starts every phase worker itself. Do not choose or start the next phase. When your reading is complete and its comment is posted, end this step with the `legion` tool: `op: "handoff_complete"`, a `summary`, and `verdict: "pass"` or `verdict: "fail"`. The verdict lets the daemon choose the next transition. A `fail` may name the earlier step whose work must change with `return_to`: that step's place in your task's `Steps:` line, 1 for its first. A `fail` that names none sends the work back to the task's first step of the type implementation before yours. Where no such step stands before yours, a `fail` that names none stops the task, and its owner decides. A refusal records nothing, so correct the call and complete again. `STEP_VERDICT_REQUIRED` is a completion with no verdict, `RETURN_TO_NEEDS_FAIL` a `return_to` on a pass, and `INVALID_RETURN_TO` a place below 1. `RETURN_REFUSED` is a step the task cannot go back to. A `pass` while an issue beneath yours is still in the workflow is refused `SIGNOFF_CHILD_IN_WORKFLOW`, naming each one. Tell your architect with `envoy_publish` to its role topic, and end the turn with a `WAITING` line naming them. Send the same report again once each has left the workflow.

This step writes no handoff record, so write none with `dispatch handoff-write`. The `legion` tool reports the commit your workspace stands on, `@-`, as the commit you read (`jj -R "$LEGION_WORKSPACE" log -r @- --no-graph -T commit_id` prints it). Read the work at that commit and name it in your comment. Where a step stands before yours and `@-` is not the head it passed, a commit landed after that step. In that case, tell your architect with `envoy_publish` to its role topic. Then end the turn with a line that starts `WAITING`, rather than completing at a head no earlier step read.

You never call `sign_off`: it is the owner's act on a step `acceptance:owner`, not yours, and your verdict is what ends this step. Send the work back with your `fail`, never with `request_backward_move`.
